01 · Brand Story

Authentic Philly street food, built for Saudi Arabia.

Before BLY'S was a restaurant, it was a sandwich shared among family and friends. The founder spent years perfecting an authentic Philly cheesesteak at home. In 2021 the vision was born — no shortcuts, no compromises, Black Angus beef in every sandwich.

08 · Sound & Music

Set the volume to the street.

Music is part of the BLY'S identity — urban hip-hop, R&B and street beats that echo Riyadh nights. The level rises with the day, but never overpowers conversation or the kitchen. إيقاع المدينة، بصوت مضبوط.

55–60dB

Opening · Daytime

Low-key urban. Relaxed beats that warm the room without filling it.

60–65dB

Lunch · Afternoon

Steady hip-hop & R&B groove. Energy you feel, talk you can still hear.

65–70dB

Evening · Riyadh Nights

Peak street-culture energy. Loud enough to move, never to shout over.

Sounds right
  • Curated hip-hop, R&B and urban beats — on-brand street culture.
  • Playlists refreshed monthly and kept consistent across branches.
  • Guests can talk without raising their voice.
Turn it off
  • Music fully off during prayer times (أوقات الصلاة).
  • Music off at closing while the last guests finish up.
  • No explicit lyrics, off-brand genres, or radio ads.
