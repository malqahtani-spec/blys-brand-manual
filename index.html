1.1 · Brand Overview
Built around the cheesesteak itself.

BLY'S is Saudi Arabia's original cheesesteak-focused restaurant. While competitors offer cheesesteaks as one menu item, BLY'S was built around the cheesesteak itself — premium ingredients, bold flavor, and a genuine connection to Philadelphia's most iconic sandwich.

From day one the rule was simple: no shortcuts, no compromises, no watered-down version of the original. Carefully selected Black Angus beef, authentic preparation, and an unmistakably urban identity in every branch.

BLY'S Brand Manual · 202605
1.2 · The Beginning
From a home kitchen to Riyadh's streets.

Before BLY'S was a restaurant, it was a sandwich shared among family and friends. The founder spent years perfecting an authentic Philly cheesesteak at home. What began as a personal obsession quickly became a favorite among everyone around him.

Family gatherings, casual meals, and late-night cravings revolved around the same question: when are you making cheesesteaks again? That question became a business. In 2021 the vision for BLY'S was born.

1.4 · Recognizing the Gap
A market with no specialist.

The Market Gap

Saudi Arabia offered many American-inspired food concepts, but no restaurant was dedicated entirely to the Philly cheesesteak — and none focused on preparing it the authentic way.

The Decision

The founder partnered with like-minded entrepreneurs who shared one belief: if they were going to build a cheesesteak restaurant, it had to be done properly.

3.8 · Print, Digital & Accessibility
Reproduce it right

Digital (RGB / HEX)

Use HEX values on screen, web, app, and social. Deep Green #002D12 backgrounds with Warm White #F2EEE8 text. Orange #FF9518 for CTAs and links only.

Print (CMYK / Pantone)

Use CMYK for process print and the nearest Pantone for spot color on packaging and signage. Confirm green on uncoated vs coated stock — it shifts.

Accessibility — pass
  • Warm White on Deep Green — ~13:1, AAA
  • Charcoal on Warm White — ~18:1, AAA
  • Orange on Charcoal for accents — ~8:1
Accessibility — avoid
  • Orange text on Warm White — fails body text
  • Deep Green text on Charcoal — too low
  • Warm Gray for small body text on light
